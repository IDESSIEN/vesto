1. Executive Summary

Seventy percent of SME exporters in sub-Saharan Africa and South-East Asia are rejected for trade finance by traditional banks. The gap is not credit risk — it is infrastructure. Vesto replaces the correspondent-bank stack with a three-party onchain protocol: verified seller, lender deploying capital, Arc settling USDC in under one second.

Vesto Thesis

Vesto is a non-custodial invoice financing marketplace that connects verified SME exporters (Sellers) with yield-seeking capital providers (Lenders) through a USDC-denominated escrow contract on Arc. Sellers receive immediate cash advances against unpaid invoices. Lenders earn 12–18% net APY on short-duration, asset-backed trade receivables. Platform operations are governed by an Admin role that manages KYC verification and dispute resolution.

1.1 Problem

SME exporters in frontier markets issue invoices with 30–90 day payment terms. During that window, working capital is locked — unable to fund new production, pay suppliers, or scale. Traditional invoice factoring requires weeks of bank paperwork, charges 5–8% fees, and excludes exporters without formal banking relationships.

1.2 Solution

Vesto digitises the trade receivable, verifies the seller via a tiered KYC process, lists the invoice on an onchain marketplace, and settles the advance in USDC within 24 hours of admin approval. Repayment happens when the buyer pays the seller, who then marks the invoice settled, triggering lender payout from escrow.

1.3 North-Star Metric

Total USDC advanced per month — the single number that captures whether Vesto is actually putting capital in the hands of exporters. Supporting metrics: lender deployment rate (%), average time-to-advance (hours), and platform fee revenue.

2. User Personas

Amara Osei
The Seller
Profile

Agri-exporter, Ghana. Supplies fresh produce to EU supermarket chains. Issues €40k–€200k invoices on 60-day terms. Has a smartphone, M-Pesa, and a USDC wallet.

Job to be Done

Convert unpaid invoices into USDC within 24 hours to fund the next harvest cycle without selling equity or pledging land.

Frustrations

Banks demand 3 years of audited accounts. Factoring firms charge 8%. Wire transfers take 5 days. No bank account in buyer's country.

Priya Nambiar
The Lender
Profile

Singapore-based DeFi investor. Holds USDC across Base and Arc. Earns 5% in traditional yield vaults. Seeks higher yield on short-duration, real-world assets.

Job to be Done

Deploy idle USDC at 12–18% net APY on 30–90 day terms with transparent risk scoring and onchain settlement proof.

Frustrations

DeFi yields have compressed. TradFi RWA products require KYC to the protocol level. No transparency on underlying asset quality.

Kwame Asante
The Admin
Profile

Vesto operations team. Reviews KYC submissions, approves invoices, manages disputes, monitors platform health. Works from a desktop admin dashboard.

Job to be Done

Review and approve sellers and invoices within 2 hours. Maintain audit trail. Resolve disputes fairly and quickly. Monitor platform risk in real time.

Frustrations

Manual review queues pile up. No audit trail on decisions. Disputes have no structured resolution workflow. Analytics are scattered across spreadsheets.

5.2 Seller Metrics

  • Tier 1 KYC completion rate: target ≥70% of sign-ups completing Tier 1 within 7 days
  • Invoice approval rate: target ≥85% of submitted invoices approved by admin
  • Repeat submission rate: target ≥60% of sellers submit a second invoice within 45 days of first payout
  • Payout claim latency: target <4 hours from funding to claim transaction

5.3 Lender Metrics

  • Capital deployment rate: target ≥80% of marketplace invoices funded within 48 hours of listing
  • Net APY delivered: target 12–18% net of 2.8% platform fee
  • Lender retention (90-day): target ≥70% of lenders reinvest within 90 days
  • Average portfolio duration: target 45–60 days weighted average

5.4 Admin Metrics

  • KYC review time: target <2 hours median from submission to decision
  • Dispute resolution time: target <48 hours median from flag to resolution
  • False positive KYC rejection rate: target <5% (rejections appealed and overturned)

6. Out of Scope — V1

  • Fiat on/off ramp integration (users must arrive with USDC)
  • Mobile native app (iOS / Android)
  • Multi-signature admin actions
  • Automated KYC via third-party API (manual review in V1)
  • Pooled lender capital (each invoice funded by a single lender in V1)
  • Cross-chain invoice funding (Arc only in V1)
  • Secondary market for funded positions
  • Governance / protocol token
  • Borrower credit scoring API (risk score is admin-set in V1)

7. Risk & Mitigation

RiskProbabilityImpactMitigation
Seller default / buyer non-paymentMediumHighTiered KYC, invoice verification, admin flag workflow, dispute resolution. Lenders accept this risk explicitly in risk disclosure.
USDC blocklist affecting lenderLowHighPull-claim architecture — lender initiates withdrawal; contract does not push. No lender can brick the contract.
Smart contract exploitLowCriticalReentrancyGuard, SafeERC20, pull-claim, admin pre-registration, third-party audit before mainnet.
KYC document fraudMediumHighCleanverse AI document analysis, manual admin review, two-tier verification with escalating limits.
Regulatory classification (securities)MediumHighLegal opinion required before mainnet launch in regulated jurisdictions. V1 testnet only.
Arc network downtimeLowMediumFrontend degrades gracefully; off-chain metadata preserved in Supabase; invoices re-listable after recovery.
Admin key compromiseLowCriticalMulti-sig admin wallet for V2. V1 uses single admin key with hardware wallet requirement.
Low lender liquidity at launchHighMediumBootstrapped lender program: team deploys initial capital pool. Invoice listing delayed until minimum pool reached.
