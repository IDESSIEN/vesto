1. Seller Journey

The seller journey has two phases: onboarding (sign-up → KYC → credit limit unlocked) and operational (invoice submission → admin review → marketplace → funded → claim payout). These phases are separate and sequential — a seller cannot submit invoices until Tier 1 KYC is approved.

Seller Onboarding Phase — Sign-up to credit unlock
1
Land on Seller Sign Up
Chooses "Seller" on role picker. Completes 4-step form: full name, phone, business name, operating country, category.
Screen: SellerSignUp
2
Arrive at Seller Dashboard (unverified)
Balance hero shows 0 USDC. Progress checklist: Tier 1 = active, credit limit = $0. Invoice submission blocked by lock gate.
Screen: SellerDashboard
3
Start Tier 1 KYC
Cleanverse AI document analysis. Uploads national ID or passport photo. Selects country. Sees animated AI analysis badge.
Screen: Tier1Verification
4
Enter Tier 2 KYC (optional, for $5,000 limit)
Video selfie with live viewfinder. Upload tax PIN + 3-month bank statement. Both documents verified server-side.
Screen: Tier2Verification
Skip path: stay at Tier 1 ($500 limit)
5
Verification submitted — awaiting admin review
VerificationInProgress screen shows step timeline. Seller can check status at any time. Typical review: <2 hours.
Screen: VerificationInProgress → SellerStatus
6
Admin approves → Credit limit unlocked
Dashboard updates: Tier 1 badge appears, credit limit = $500. Invoice submission now available. Progress step 1 ticked.
Screen: SellerDashboard (updated)
Seller Operational Phase — Invoice to payout
1
Submit invoice
Enters buyer name, tax ID, country, invoice amount, due date. Uploads PDF. Payout breakdown ribbon shows: face value, advance (85–90%), platform fee (2%), net payout.
Screen: SubmitInvoice
Block: exceeds credit limit → upgrade to Tier 2 prompt
2
Invoice enters admin review queue
Status = pending_admin_approval. Visible on dashboard in "Invoice ledger" tab. Dashboard stat tiles update.
Screen: SellerDashboard
3
Admin approves → Invoice listed on marketplace
Status = published_marketplace. Invoice now visible to lenders. Seller dashboard shows "On market" counter.
Screen: SellerDashboard (status updated)
4
Lender funds invoice onchain
VestoEscrow.fundInvoice() called. Status = funded. USDC moves from lender to escrow. Seller sees "In escrow" capital on dashboard. Arc settlement: <1 second.
Onchain: VestoEscrow · SellerDashboard updated
5
Buyer pays seller off-platform
Buyer settles invoice via wire/SWIFT/local payment. Seller marks invoice settled in dashboard ("Mark settled" button).
Screen: SellerDashboard → InvoiceRow button
6
Admin confirms repayment → markRepaid
Admin reviews settlement confirmation. Calls markRepaid on contract. USDC moves from escrow to seller's pendingClaims. Lender payout also added.
Screen: InvoiceOversightTable (Admin)
7
Seller claims payout onchain
Green "Payout ready" banner appears on dashboard. Seller clicks "Claim USDC" → claimPayout() called → USDC arrives in wallet. Status = repaid.
Screen: SellerDashboard · Onchain: VestoEscrow.claimPayout()

2. Lender Journey

Lender Full journey — Sign-up to capital deployed
1
Lender sign-up
Chooses "Lender". Completes profile: full name, email, account type (individual/institutional), target allocation, auto-invest preference.
Screen: LenderSignUp
2
Risk disclosure gate
Must read and accept risk disclosure before accessing marketplace. Cannot skip. Checkbox confirmation required.
Screen: RiskDisclosure
3
Guided tour (optional)
3-minute walkthrough of marketplace mechanics, risk scoring, and yield split. Skippable.
Screen: MarketplaceGuidedTour
4
Browse marketplace
Sees live invoice cards. Each card shows: seller name, buyer, one-liner thesis, risk meter, gross/net yield split, term, advance amount. Filter by category, yield, or term length.
Screen: MarketplaceBrowse
5
Fund invoice onchain
Click "Fund $X,XXX" → Funding modal opens. Reviews deal facts (gross APY / fee / net APY). Clicks "Deploy $X,XXX USDC". Step 1: approve USDC. Step 2: fundInvoice(). Arc confirms in <1 second.
Screen: FundingModal · Onchain: VestoEscrow
Block: insufficient USDC balance → "Get test USDC" prompt
6
Monitor portfolio
Lender Portfolio shows: deployed capital, gross yield, net APY tile (split display), active positions list with per-invoice yield breakdown. Each position row shows one-liner thesis.
Screen: LenderPortfolio
7
Invoice repaid → lender payout claimable
pendingClaims updated onchain. Lender sees green "Claim" banner. Withdraws USDC to wallet. Optional: auto-reinvest toggle re-deploys into next available invoice.
Screen: LenderPortfolio · Onchain: claimPayout()

3. Admin Journey

Admin Daily operations — Login to oversight
1
Admin login with 2FA
Email must be @vesto.finance domain. Enter 6-digit passcode (demo: 123456). Invalid domain or wrong length blocked immediately.
Screen: AdminLogin2FA
2
Analytics overview (landing)
KPI tiles: total volume, active liquidity, default rate, avg APY. Sparkline charts. Sector breakdown. Arc protocol health card.
Screen: AnalyticsOverview
3
Review KYC verification queue
T1/T2 avatar tiles. Review document, add admin note, approve or reject with undo toast. Approved seller → sellers registry updated → credit limit unlocked in seller's dashboard.
Screen: VerificationQueue
4
Review invoice oversight table
All submitted invoices. Status filter. Flag icon triggers reason modal. Flag reason badges on table rows. Approve: publishes to marketplace. Reject: returns to seller with reason.
Screen: InvoiceOversightTable
5
Resolve disputes
Flagged invoices escalate here. Admin sees both parties' claims. Two actions: "Refund Lender" (returns USDC from escrow to lender) or "Pay Seller" (releases USDC to seller pendingClaims).
Screen: DisputeResolution
6
Mark invoice repaid
Seller has notified buyer paid. Admin verifies offline confirmation (bank receipt, SWIFT advice). Calls markRepaid. USDC moves to pendingClaims for both seller and lender.
Screen: InvoiceOversightTable · Onchain: markRepaid()

4. Invoice Lifecycle State Machine

Six states

Registered
Admin pre-registered on contract. Not yet visible to lenders. Awaiting admin invoice review.
Published
Admin approved. Live on marketplace. Visible to all lenders. Accepting funding.
Funded
Lender called fundInvoice(). USDC in escrow. Seller notified. Awaiting buyer payment.
Repaid
Admin confirmed buyer paid. markRepaid() called. Both pendingClaims updated. Terminal state.
Disputed
Seller or lender raised dispute. Escrow frozen. Admin resolving. Leads to repaid or refunded.
Flagged
Admin flagged during review. Reason recorded. Returned to seller for correction or rejected.

Eight transitions

pending_admin_approval → registered  | Admin approves invoice + calls registerInvoice()
pending_admin_approval → flagged     | Admin flags invoice with reason
registered           → published_marketplace | Admin publishes to marketplace
published_marketplace  → funded        | Lender calls fundInvoice()
published_marketplace  → flagged       | Admin flags post-listing
funded                → repaid        | Admin calls markRepaid() after buyer confirms
funded                → disputed       | Seller or lender raises dispute
disputed              → repaid        | Admin resolves (pay seller or refund lender)

6. Error & Edge-Case Flows

ScenarioWhereHandling
Lender: insufficient USDC balanceFundingModalRed banner: "You need $X,XXX USDC. Get test USDC from Arc Studio sidebar." CTA disabled.
Seller: credit limit exceededSubmitInvoiceInput field turns error-red. "Advance of $X exceeds your $Y available credit. Upgrade to Tier 2 for $5,000 limit." Submit button disabled.
KYC: verification rejectedSellerStatusRed state on Tier step. Reason shown. "Re-submit" CTA routes to Tier1 or Tier2.
Transaction: wallet rejectedFundingModal / ClaimPayoutError state in modal: "Transaction cancelled in wallet." Reset + retry CTA.
Transaction: contract revertFundingModalError state: decoded revert reason shown if available. "Try again" resets to idle.
Admin 2FA: wrong email domainAdminLogin2FAInline error: "Admin access requires a @vesto.finance email address." Field highlighted red.
Admin 2FA: wrong passcode lengthAdminLogin2FAButton disabled until exactly 6 digits entered. "Enter exactly 6 digits" hint shown.
Dispute raised on funded invoiceInvoiceOversightTableInvoice status → disputed. Escrow frozen. Admin routed to DisputeResolution screen. Timer starts.
Wallet disconnected mid-flowAny onchain screenConnectKitButton appears in place of disabled action button. State preserved — user reconnects and continues.
